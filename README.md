## Overview

Corporate Social Responsibility activities are often managed using spreadsheets, emails, and disconnected systems.

This makes it difficult for organizations to:

- Manage NGO and CSR partners
- Track compliance documents
- Create and manage CSR programs
- Coordinate employee volunteering
- Track attendance and volunteer hours
- Control CSR budgets and commitments
- Verify beneficiaries and social impact
- Monitor CSR KPIs
- Generate management reports
- Take timely corrective action

### Our Approach

We built a centralized CSR platform on **ServiceNow** that connects these activities into one controlled lifecycle:

**Plan → Validate → Approve → Execute → Measure → Verify → Analyze → Improve**

Instead of treating CSR activities as separate processes, our platform connects the data and workflows across the entire CSR lifecycle.
